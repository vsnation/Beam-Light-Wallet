## Battle Mechanics

MemeClash runs on a simple but devastating loop. Every **24 hours (~1440 blocks)**, one team wins and the other gets burned.

### Step 1: Buy Your Team

Pick $CHAD or $GIGA. Buy with BEAM through the app. 95% of tokens go to your wallet. The other 5% goes to your team's battle treasury — the war chest.

### Step 2: Wait for the Bell

For 24 hours, both teams buy, strategize, and trash-talk. The more your team buys, the bigger the treasury and the more BEAM flows into your team's DEX pool.

### Step 3: Round Ends

After 1440 blocks, the smart contract checks: which team's DEX pool attracted more BEAM? Not price — actual capital flow. More BEAM in = you win.

### Step 4: The Burn

Loser's entire treasury gets sold on DEX for BEAM. That BEAM buys winner tokens. Those tokens get permanently destroyed. 85% burned, 10% ecosystem, 5% caller reward.

### Step 5: Next Round

Anyone can start the next round. The cycle repeats every single day. No breaks. No mercy.

Battles can be triggered by **anyone**. No admin pressing a button. When a round ends, any player can call the checkpoint. First one to do it earns **5% of all the BEAM** from the loser's treasury sale. Real money for one click.

> The smart contract checks the DEX. The math decides the winner. No admins. No oracles. No BS.

### Draw Rules

Sometimes both teams show up equally strong — or equally weak. If both pools had the same growth, or both shrank?

**It's a DRAW.** Both treasuries get burned. No carryover. The stakes reset and the next round starts fresh.

### Anti-Rug

The 5% trade fee means every purchase grows the team's treasury automatically. Even if big holders dump, the next round is already funded. The game resets every 24 hours — there's always a reason to play.
