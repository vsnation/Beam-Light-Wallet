## Image Guidelines for All Posts

- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 for tweets (1200×675px or 1600×900px)
- **Color palette:** Dark backgrounds (#0a0e17 or #111827), CHAD green (#25c2a0), GIGA purple (#a855f7)
- **Style:** Futuristic, cyberpunk, clean digital art — NOT cartoon, NOT pixel art
- **NO text in images** — let the tweet do the talking. Images are pure visual.
- **Consistent branding:** Every image should feel like it's from the same universe
- **No faces** — use silhouettes, energy effects, abstract shapes
- **High contrast** — dark backgrounds with bright neon accents
